## Groundwork Simulator Xbox Controller Support: The Real Question

Many players asking about Groundwork Simulator Xbox are really asking a narrower question: can I play this with a controller? That matters because the answer often predicts how painful a console port would be.

Operating an excavator means managing a boom, a stick, a bucket, and a rotating cab, plus track movement — all at once. On a keyboard and mouse that maps comfortably to many keys and precise mouse aim. On a gamepad, it needs clever layering, modifier buttons, or a simplified control preset. Simulation developers solve this in different ways, and some choose not to solve it at all.

At the time of writing, there is no confirmed gamepad support detail for Groundwork Simulator, and no confirmation that the PC build ships with an Xbox controller layout. If controller play is a dealbreaker for you, wait for an official statement rather than assuming.

### Tips for New Operators

Whenever you do start digging — on PC now or on a future console port — a few habits pay off immediately:

- **Test before you commit.** Sample the ground in several spots before you move serious dirt. Ore seams are not where the prettiest terrain is.
- **Build a wash station early.** Gold you cannot process is just gravel with ambition.
- **Upgrade capacity before speed.** A bigger bucket or hopper compounds; raw engine power does not.
- **Play co-op with defined roles.** One operator digs, one hauls, one washes. Four people doing the same job is chaos.
- **Flatten your staging area.** Deformable terrain means your own ruts will slow you down later.
