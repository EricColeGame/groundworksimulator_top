## The Roles That Make a Co-Op Site Run

The fastest way to ruin a shared dig is to have two players do the same task. Groundwork Simulator multiplayer works best when each person owns a link in the chain from hillside to gold.

| Role | Main machine or tool | Core job | Why it matters in co-op |
|---|---|---|---|
| Excavator operator | Excavator with bucket | Cut trenches, load material | Keeps pay dirt flowing without pause |
| Haul driver | Truck and trailer | Move material to the wash plant | Eliminates the excavator's travel time |
| Wash plant operator | Wash plant, sluice boxes, shaking table | Process concentrate | Converts dug material into actual recovery |
| Water technician | Pumps and hoses | Keep water supplied | A wash plant without water is scenery |
| Test driller | Drill and test holes | Find gold-rich ground | Stops the crew from excavating barren dirt |

The excavator-plus-transport split is the classic pairing the developers highlight: one player digs, the other keeps material moving between the digging area and the wash plant. Once a third player joins, putting them on water and testing pays off quickly, because both tasks are easy to neglect when you are focused on a bucket.

A useful rule of thumb: assign roles before anyone climbs into a machine. Swapping seats mid-session sounds harmless, but every swap is dead time on a site where the terrain is constantly changing under you.

## Setting Up a Groundwork Simulator Multiplayer Session

Good sessions are boring to start and efficient to finish. Run this checklist before the first scoop.

| Step | Action | Why it saves time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finish the story tutorial solo first | You learn digging, hydraulics, and washing without burning a friend's session |
| 2 | Agree on one worksite and one goal | Prevents two players digging two unrelated holes |
| 3 | Assign machines and roles out loud | Avoids duplicate work and idle equipment |
| 4 | Set up water before mass excavation | The wash plant cannot run dry mid-run |
| 5 | Drill and test a few holes first | Confirms the ground is worth the fuel |
| 6 | Keep chat open the entire session | Terrain changes fast; call out slopes and loads |
| 7 | Park equipment clear of the cut | Prevents a loaded truck from sliding into a trench |

The tutorial point deserves emphasis. The Early Access build includes a story mode that walks players through the core mechanics, so the person hosting should complete it before inviting anyone. Teaching a friend how hydraulic resistance feels while they sit in a machine on your shared map is a poor use of everyone's evening.

## Coordination Mistakes That Cost Real Time

Most failed co-op sessions are not caused by bad machine handling. They are caused by unspoken assumptions.

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Two players digging the same cut | Collisions, wasted fuel, blocked routes | Draw the site plan before engines start |
| Nobody owns water | Wash plant stalls with a full hopper | Assign a water technician early |
| Hauling before testing | You move dirt that holds no gold | Drill first, then commit the crew |
| Overloading the trailer on slopes | Lost loads and stuck trucks | Take the long, flatter route with a heavy machine aboard |
| Silent chat | One player widens a trench the other needed | Narrate what you are about to change |

A few habits make an immediate difference:

- **Call your cuts.** Say "cutting the north slope" before the bucket moves, not after.
- **Improve the ground before you fight it.** Mud and steep grades are easier to fix with a bucket than to muscle through with a loaded truck.
- **Stage equipment near the wash plant.** Fewer long hauls means more scoops per hour.
- **End sessions at a clean stopping point.** Park machines on flat ground so the next session does not start with a recovery job.

## Performance and Early Access Realities

Groundwork Simulator's soil and water simulation is heavy on the processor, and the developers say so directly — the physics load is demanding even though the visuals look modest. Co-op does not remove that load from your machine, so check your hardware before you commit to a long session.

- **Minimum (Windows):** Windows 10, Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 2600, 16 GB RAM, GTX 1070 with 8 GB VRAM, DirectX 12, 10 GB storage.
- **Recommended (Windows):** Windows 11, Intel Core i5-12400 or AMD Ryzen 5 5600, 24 GB RAM, RTX 3060 or RX 6600 with 8 GB VRAM.
- **macOS:** Apple M4 minimum with 16 GB RAM; Apple M5 Pro recommended with 24 GB RAM.

Second, the base price is not planned to change when the game leaves Early Access, so there is no financial reason to wait on Groundwork Simulator multiplayer if you already have a friend ready to dig.
