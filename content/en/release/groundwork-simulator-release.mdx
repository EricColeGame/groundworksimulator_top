## What the Early Access Build Actually Contains

The developers describe the current state as "most of the features planned for the full release" — a claim worth unpacking, because it separates this launch from the thin vertical slices that often define Early Access debuts.

### The core loop

You dig, haul, and process. Trenches and hillside cuts permanently alter the terrain mesh, so the route you carve on day one becomes the road you drive on day ten. Material goes from bucket to trailer to wash plant, where sluice boxes and a shaking table concentrate the pay dirt. Drilling and test holes come before committing equipment to a spot, which rewards prospecting over blind excavation.

### The systems layer

| System | What it does | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Excavation physics | Hydraulic resistance and bucket fill simulation | Makes digging a skill, not a button press |
| Terrain deformation | Persistent changes to ground geometry | Your worksite evolves and can become harder to navigate |
| Water routing | Pumps and hoses feed the wash plant | Adds logistics planning to the mining loop |
| Transport | Trailer loading and truck hauling | Turns machine relocation into a driving challenge |
| Traction model | Mud, slopes, and heavy loads affect grip | Forces route decisions and ground improvement |
| Online co-op | Shared site, split roles | One player digs, another hauls |

One transparency note worth flagging: the developers disclose that generative AI tools were used to assist with some assets. That is stated plainly on the store page rather than buried, and players who care about asset provenance should factor it into their purchase decision.

### Solo versus co-op

Solo play is fully supported, and the story mode doubles as a guided tutorial that walks you through the mechanics before turning you loose. Co-op is where the systems interlock, though — running an excavator while a friend keeps material moving between the dig face and the wash plant mirrors how a real site operates, and it removes the constant machine-swapping that slows solo sessions.

## Tips for Your First Worksite

- **Prospect before you commit heavy iron.** Drill and test for gold-rich ground first. Moving a wash plant is expensive in time, and setting it up over barren dirt wastes an entire session.
- **Build your roads early.** Because terrain deformation is permanent, the slope you cut on hour one becomes the slope you climb on hour five. Plan haul routes with loaded trucks in mind, not empty ones.
- **Solve water before you dig.** Pumps and hoses need routing across the site, and a wash plant without supply is dead weight. Lay the line, confirm flow, then start moving material.
- **Improve traction instead of fighting it.** Mud and grades punish a loaded trailer. Sometimes the correct next job is grading the ground beneath your wheels.
- **Split roles in co-op.** One player on the excavator and one on transport beats two players competing for the same machine.
