## What Is Groundwork Simulator Early Access?

Groundwork Simulator early access is here, and for anyone who has ever wanted to run a small gold-mining operation with a full fleet of heavy machinery, it is a notable moment. The excavation and gold-recovery sim launched on Steam on October 22, 2026, giving players an immediately playable slice of a game that is still being built. Why does that matter? Because the Groundwork Simulator early access launch arrives with a 20% introductory discount, a clearly stated development window of roughly three to six months, and a developer promise that the base price will not change at full release — three details that make buying in early a much easier call.

The pitch is straightforward: you operate excavators and support equipment, reshape a hillside scoop by scoop, haul material across your worksite, and run it through a wash plant to recover gold. You can do it alone or split the job with friends in online co-op.

The developers describe the current build as having its gameplay foundation fully in place, with the Early Access period used to add more maps and vehicles and to let player feedback steer additional features. In other words, this is not a tech demo — it is a working mining loop that will grow.

**Core pillars of the current build:**

- Terrain that permanently changes with every bucket you dig
- An excavation engine with simulated hydraulic resistance
- A full dirt-to-gold processing chain, from test holes to shaking table
- Pump-and-hose water routing for the wash plant
- Truck-and-trailer transport of heavy machinery between sites
- Online co-op for dividing labor across the worksite

### The mining loop, step by step

1. **Prospect.** Explore the terrain for ground that looks gold-rich and drill test holes to confirm.
2. **Set up.** Position your wash plant and run water to it with pumps and hoses.
3. **Excavate.** Dig promising material, managing bucket angle and hydraulic power as resistance builds.
4. **Haul.** Move material from the cut to the plant, keeping the circuit running.
5. **Process.** Run material through sluice boxes, then finish concentrate on the shaking table.
6. **Rebuild.** Reshape the site as you go — the ground you leave behind becomes the base for the next job.

That sixth step is the quiet star of the design. Because terrain deformation is permanent, poor planning compounds. A sloppy access ramp today becomes a traction problem tomorrow.

## System Requirements, Performance, and Getting Started Tips

The simulation is far heavier than the visuals suggest. The developers explicitly warn that detailed soil and water physics place a heavy CPU load, and the memory requirements reflect that.

| Platform | Setting | CPU | RAM | GPU | OS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Windows | Minimum | Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600 | 16 GB | NVIDIA GTX 1070 (8 GB) | Windows 10 |
| Windows | Recommended | Intel Core i5-12400 / AMD Ryzen 5 5600 | 24 GB | RTX 3060 (8 GB) / RX 6600 (8 GB) | Windows 11 |
| macOS | Minimum | Apple M4 | 16 GB | Apple M4 | Tahoe or later |
| macOS | Recommended | Apple M5 Pro | 24 GB | Apple M5 Pro | Tahoe or later |

Both platforms require a 64-bit processor and 10 GB of storage, with DirectX 12 on Windows. The 24 GB recommended memory figure is on the high side for a simulation title, so if you are near the minimum, expect to trade visual settings for simulation headroom.

**Practical tips for your first sessions:**

- **Learn on the tutorial map before chasing gold.** Story mode teaches bucket control and machine handling without punishing you for wasted digs.
- **Build your water route early.** Pumps and hoses are easy to place badly; laying them out before the site gets crowded saves rework.
- **Improve ground before hauling heavy loads.** Mud and slopes punish a loaded trailer, and sometimes the real job is fixing the road.
- **Keep the digging face close to the plant.** Longer hauls mean more trips through bad terrain and more chances to get stuck.
- **Watch traction, not speed.** Momentum is not your friend on a wet grade with a full bucket.
